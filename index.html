Honest notes

  • Font. The mockup uses Segoe UI, a Windows system font that cannot be served on the web. It is replaced with Selawik, Microsoft's open replacement under SIL OFL, metric-compatible with Segoe UI. The compatibility is checked against the mockup: where the font size is right, line boxes match to the pixel. The remaining difference on the regular weight is 2–3% of line length.
  • Inconsistencies inside the mockup. The mobile and desktop frames disagree in places: different lists in the third band, accordion answers in different letter case, two title lines on mobile set in different sizes (36 and 32), footer links on mobile without underline and with a different conjunction. All of it is reproduced frame by frame rather than unified: a difference from the mockup is a bug, even when it looks nicer.
  • Status bar in the mobile export. The clock, wifi and battery at the top of the frame export are the Figma frame, not the page. In the overlay they never match, and that is how it should be.
  • Widths in between. A pixel comparison is only possible at two widths, because the mockup has no other frames. Between them the layout is fluid, and that is an engineering decision rather than a match to a picture.
